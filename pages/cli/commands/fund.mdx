# `fund`

Fund your Irys account with the specified amount of tokens.

<Callout type="info" emoji="ℹ️">
Not all calls to `fund` will post immediately to your account, some blockchains are faster than others. When funding with AR, it can take upwards of 40 minutes before the balance post. For MATIC, ETH and SOL, balances will usually post in < 10s.
</Callout>

**Parameters**

<div className="-mt-6" />

- `-n`: The network to check. Use `mainnet`.
- `-t`: The [token](/overview/supported-tokens) to use when funding.
- `-w`: Your [private key](/cli#using-private-keys).
- `--provider-url`: Optional RPC URL to use. 

## Example

```console
irys fund 1000000000000000 \
  -t ethereum \
  -w bf20......c9885307
```
